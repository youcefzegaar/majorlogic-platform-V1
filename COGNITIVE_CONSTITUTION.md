# MajorLogic: Cognitive Constitution (v1.0)

> "A decision is not a calculation of the best; it is the navigation of the right trade-off."

This document serves as the supreme logical authority for the MajorLogic platform. Every algorithm, AI prompt, and configuration must adhere to the principles, units, and laws defined herein to maintain **Cognitive Integrity**.

---

## I. The Fundamental Philosophy

### 1. Decision as Trade-off
We reject the notion of "simple optimization." In a world of finite resources, every choice involves a sacrifice. The goal of MajorLogic is to identify, quantify, and explain the most sustainable trade-off for a specific human intent.

### 2. Conflict as the Engine
Conflict (the collision of competing desires) is not an error; it is the starting point of a decision. A system that avoids conflict is a search engine; a system that navigates conflict is a decision engine.

### 3. Wisdom over Intelligence
Intelligence is finding a path; Wisdom is choosing the right path among many painful alternatives. Our system prioritizes "Cognitive Wisdom"—the alignment of sacrifices with the user's hierarchy of values.

---

## II. Primitive Cognitive Units (The Atoms)

All domain configurations must be composed of these primitive units:

| Unit | Semantic Definition | Technical Representation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dimension** | A measurable attribute of reality (e.g., Weight, Price). | A leaf node in the decision tree. |
| **Intent** | A higher-order human goal (e.g., "Creative Work"). | A root node or weighting profile. |
| **Constraint** | A boundary of acceptability. | A threshold or limit function. |
| **Trade-off** | The relationship between two competing dimensions. | A mathematical conflict map. |
| **Gate** | An absolute barrier (Non-negotiable). | A hard constraint (Binary pass/fail). |
| **Signal** | A subtle indicator of quality or fit. | A low-weight contextual modifier. |
| **Recovery** | A deliberate relaxation of logic to evade collapse. | A fallback state triggered by zero-results. |

---

## III. The Four Laws of Cognitive Physics

### 1. The Law of Sacrifice
*Every optimization introduces a measurable sacrifice.* 
The system must never claim "no downsides." It must always quantify the **Sacrifice Vector** (what was lost to gain what was won).

### 2. The Law of Conflict Emergence
*High-value intents naturally generate dimensional conflicts.* 
The more important an intent is, the more likely the system will encounter contradictions (e.g., High Performance vs. Low Cost). The system must expose these conflicts rather than hide them.

### 3. The Law of Stability
*A decision is stable only when the sacrifices align with the intent's hierarchy.* 
A decision that sacrifices a "Gate" or a high-priority dimension is considered **Unstable** and must be flagged for manual review or narrative intervention.

### 4. The Law of Semantic Drift (Collapse)
*Excessive recovery leads to a loss of meaning.* 
If the system relaxes more than 30% of its constraints to find a result, it has suffered a **Cognitive Collapse**. The resulting recommendation is no longer a decision but a random guess.

---

## IV. Behavioral Integrity Standards

To ensure the "Mental Health" of the system, every domain must pass the **Cognitive Stress Test**:

1. **Adversarial Consistency:** The system must yield the same recommendation for the same trade-off profile, regardless of input order.
2. **Recovery Transparency:** Every activated recovery path must be disclosed in the AI Narrative. Hidden logic relaxation is a violation of integrity.
3. **Intent Preservation:** The "Essence" of the intent (defined by its Gates) must never be sacrificed. If a Gate is broken, the system must declare "No Rational Decision Possible."

---

## V. Roles & Responsibilities

*   **The Kernel:** Responsible for the mathematical accuracy of the sacrifice calculation.
*   **The AI Renderer:** Responsible for translating the Sacrifice Vector into human-centric, objective narratives. It must act as an Observer, not a Salesman.
*   **The Admin (Logic Engineer):** Responsible for mapping human wisdom into the Meta Model. They are the "Legislators" of the domain's logic.
